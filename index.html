Enes Ayaz

Ph.D. candidate in power electronics, KTH Royal Institute of Technology, Stockholm

I work on silicon-carbide traction inverters for heavy-duty electric vehicles, and on modulation methods that improve how inverters perform. I am a doctoral student in the Division of Electric Power and Energy Systems, supervised by Staffan Norrga and Hans-Peter Nee.

Before KTH, I received my B.Sc. (2019) and M.Sc. (2022) in Electrical and Electronics Engineering from Middle East Technical University, and did research on wireless power transfer at METU PowerLab.
